About Us

About Us International Architecture media is an architectural focus group specializing in the development and visualization of future spatial environments.

Founded in 2024

Specialization: Development of non-standard architectural solutions at the intersection of form and functional urbanism. The focus is on creating concepts in which the plasticity of the architectural envelope defines a new quality of space.

Scope of work includes:

  • Art objects
  • Residential houses
  • Public buildings
  • Integrated solutions for residential districts

Research projects have been presented at competitions such as "Wooden Minimalism" (Yu.Smidovich), "Beyond the Horizon. City of the Young", as well as within the fields of product design.
