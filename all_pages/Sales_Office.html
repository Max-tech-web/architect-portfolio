Sales Office

Explanatory note:

  • 1.Where is it located?
  • The architectural design of a one-story sales office building is located on a Avtozavodskaya Street in the ZIL district of Moscow
  • What is nearby?
  • The site is located in such a way that it is bounded by:
  • From the North - Likhacheva Ave.
  • From the East - an undeveloped area
  • From the West - Tyufeleva Grove Park
  • From the South - Tyufeleva Grove Park

What is the concept

The concept of the building itself reflects the features of ZIL Art, the combination of new designs, as well as preserve the historicity of this place. The office building itself does not occupy a huge space, but harmoniously enters the environment of a small park, smoothly passing under water. The office itself is somewhat like a ship. From this image comes its name "SHIP".
