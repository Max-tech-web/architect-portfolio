Exhibition Pavilion

Explanatory notes:

1.Location The architectural design of the two-storey building of the Exhibition Pavilion is located on Pushkinskaya Embankment 2.What is the concept? The pavilion building itself has a rounded shape and is made of pressed wood and metal trusses as floors. The pavilion is designed in such way that on sunny days it can show the time due to a sunroof and 12 columns that carry not only a practical purpose, but are made in the form of Roman numerals. There are also benches outside the pavilion building that act as a clock face and are also made in the form of Roman numerals. The pavilion is not a heated room and is actually end-to-end, and therefore is designed exclusively for summer and near-autumn operation.
